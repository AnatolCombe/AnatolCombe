#### 🎓 Open source — [Freinet](https://github.com/AnatolCombe/freinet)

A personal teacher that **governs learning instead of guessing it**, named after Célestin Freinet, who put a printing press in his classroom. It builds a living profile of the learner, turns any subject into a map of linked concepts, and prints a short ticket every morning, read with the coffee, whose QR feedback steers what comes next.
The language model writes; a **deterministic engine** decides mastery and review. Nothing is printed that wasn't checked first.

**Vision — Freinet Box, a solar, offline cyberdeck for schools:** a 10–25 W mini-computer on a small solar kit, local Wi-Fi with no internet, a local model that assists the teacher, and a thermal printer that hands each pupil a daily review sheet, even pupils without a phone.
